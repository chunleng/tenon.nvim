## Process
1. If re-entered from Verify Explanation, fix the issues in the verification findings artifact instead of rewriting the document; otherwise, create or update the explanation document at the `doc_path` from the subject context artifact
2. Edit the document, following rules in "Writing Rules" section and in the bullet below:
   a. Consider the following when structuring the document - they are not required sections; include a section only where it has substance, and let the structure follow the discussion:
      - Context that shaped the design
      - Decisions and their rationale
      - Tradeoffs and alternatives
      - Deliberate omissions
   b. Cover every decision in the subject context artifact with its rationale

## Writing Rules
- Prose and discussion, not steps or exhaustive listings
- One focused subject per document; bound the document around a clear why question (e.g. "why does X work this way?") and keep the subject closely bounded
- Base every factual claim and every rationale on the subject context artifact - do not invent rationale
- Describe decisions that were settled: what was chosen and why, not proposals or options to evaluate
- No instruction or technical description - if steps or exhaustive option listings appear, they belong in other documents, not here
- Judgements and opinions are allowed where they aid understanding; weigh alternatives rather than presenting one view as the only one
